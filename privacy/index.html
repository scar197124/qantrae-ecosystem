QANTRAE PRIVACY

Know where analysis happens.

Privacy in QANTRAE is tied to the job each product performs. Lightweight and local functions are kept in the browser where designed; deeper intelligence functions may require protected server-side requests.

Local-first layers

QANTRAE Lite and Sense are designed around browser-side analysis for their core local checks. Inputs used by those local checks do not need to be sent to a QANTRAE analysis server simply to produce their local result.

Deeper intelligence

Some IT capabilities require server-side intelligence or public registration/network data. Those requests are routed through bounded endpoints so provider credentials stay server-side and unsafe network targets can be rejected.

No promise beyond the build

QANTRAE should not claim that data is never transmitted when a feature genuinely depends on an external lookup. Product-specific privacy views explain the behavior of that product in more detail.

Browser storage

Where a product keeps recent state or history locally, that information belongs to the browser environment rather than a QANTRAE cloud account unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

Security and abuse controls

Server-assisted endpoints are intentionally bounded with validation, network restrictions, timeouts, redirect limits, response-size limits, origin handling, and throttling. These controls exist both to protect users and to reduce misuse of QANTRAE infrastructure.

Privacy details can evolve when features change. When a product gains a new server-assisted capability, its privacy language should be reviewed at the same time rather than treated as a one-time statement.
