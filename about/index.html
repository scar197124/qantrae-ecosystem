ABOUT QANTRAE

One ecosystem.
Different depths.

QANTRAE was built around the idea that suspicious digital moments do not all require the same response. The ecosystem gives users a progression from a quick first look to interpretation, protection, and deeper investigation.

What QANTRAE is

A family of security-focused tools that helps users examine suspicious links, domains, messages, and related technical signals. Each product has a defined lane rather than pretending one scanner should do everything.

Why four products

Lite is the quick check. Sense interprets visible warning patterns. Shield moves into stronger defensive analysis. IT provides the deepest investigation and supporting context in the family.

Our operating idea

Stop. Think. Scan. The phrase is behavioral, not decorative. QANTRAE is intended to slow down the moment between suspicion and action, then provide information that supports a better decision.

What QANTRAE is not

It is not a guarantee that a target is safe or malicious, and it does not replace professional incident response, endpoint security, or the judgment required for high-risk situations.

How the family fits together

  • Lite: fast, lightweight checking.
  • Sense: local interpretation of visible patterns and warning signs.
  • Shield: a stronger protection and evidence layer.
  • IT: deeper technical investigation using structured analysis and, where required, protected intelligence services.
The ecosystem navigation at the top is the shared QANTRAE layer. Each product may also provide its own second navigation for product-specific Home/About, Privacy, Transparency, scanner, or investigation views.
