        Clarity layer

        About QANTRAE Sense

        QANTRAE Sense is the interpretation layer of the QANTRAE family. It examines visible patterns in URLs, domains, and message text, then explains what those patterns may mean before you decide what to do next.

        What Sense checks

        • Suspicious domains, direct IPs, short links, and look-alike patterns
        • Login, payment, verification, urgency, and prize-style lure language
        • Encoded or unusual URL structure and redirect clues
        • Combinations of signals that increase concern

        What Sense does not do

        • It does not open the link for you
        • It does not contact a remote scanning service
        • It does not replace Shield or a full security product
        • It does not guarantee that a link is safe
        Sense helps you interpret. It is designed to make warning signs understandable, not to hide the reasoning behind a verdict.

        Local by design

        Sense Privacy

        Sense is designed to analyze the text you provide directly in your browser. The standalone Sense scanner does not need to send your URL, domain, or message to an external analysis service.

        No scan upload

        Your scan input stays in the browser session used by the standalone app.

        No account required

        You do not need to sign in or create a profile to use the local Sense scanner.

        Local scan memory

        Recent Sense scan history is stored locally in the browser and is not a cloud history service.

        Important: if a future QANTRAE feature uses an external service, that behavior should be identified clearly rather than being hidden inside the scanner.

        Explainable by design

        Sense Transparency

        Sense is meant to show its work. A risk score should not be a mystery number, so the scanner exposes the signals and reasoning that contribute to its assessment.

        What you can see

        • Detected warning signals
        • Threat level and score
        • Recommended actions
        • Local signal map and technical findings

        How to read it

        • More serious or combined signals raise concern
        • Confidence reflects how strongly the visible evidence supports the result
        • Low-risk does not mean guaranteed safe
        • Sense provides interpretation, not certainty
        Our rule: if Sense flags something, the user should be able to understand why.
        🛡️ All scans are performed locally on your device. Your data never leaves your device.
