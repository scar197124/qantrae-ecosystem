ABOUT SHIELD

Protection and action before the next click.

Shield is the protection layer of QANTRAE. It analyzes local risk signals, converts them into a protection posture, and gives the user a clear next action before credentials, payments, downloads, or QR destinations are trusted.

Link Protection

Shield evaluates domain construction, security characteristics, phishing language, redirects, encoded destinations, and risky file-delivery patterns before you act.

QR Protection

Camera and image decoding let you inspect a QR destination before opening it. The decoded destination can move directly into the same Shield risk engine.

Protection Posture

Results are translated into practical modes such as Proceed Carefully, Verify First, or Stop and Isolate so the score leads to an understandable action.

Where Shield Stops

Shield does not replace QANTRAE IT’s deeper evidence provenance, case memory, external intelligence correlation, or investigative workflow. IT remains the deepest layer.

SHIELD PRIVACY

Protection should not require unnecessary exposure.

The current Shield engine performs its URL scoring, category analysis, protection posture, and report generation locally in the browser. QR camera access is used only when you choose to activate it.

Local Analysis

Domain, security, phishing, and redirect/QR scoring are calculated on the device. The current build does not upload the scanned destination to a remote analysis service.

Camera Choice

Camera access is requested only when you start QR scanning, and you can stop the camera directly from Shield.

Local Reports

Shield reports are assembled locally from the scan result. Export happens only when you choose to save a report.

Future Services

If optional online intelligence is added later, the service and the data leaving the device should be identified clearly before that feature is used.

SHIELD TRANSPARENCY

Show the warning, the reason, and the action.

Shield is a local heuristic protection system. Its scores should remain understandable: the user can see the category scores, the warning signals, the overall risk classification, and the recommended protection posture.

Independent Categories

Domain, security, phishing, and redirect/QR risk are scored independently before they contribute to the overall Shield result.

Visible Signals

Detected warning indicators are shown beside the result so the user can understand what caused Shield to react.

Heuristic Limits

A low score means Shield did not detect strong local warning patterns. It does not prove a destination is safe or guarantee what happens after you leave Shield.

Blocklist Simulation

The current “Local Blocklist Simulation” remains explicitly a simulation. Persistent browser or network blocking is not claimed until that capability is actually wired.
