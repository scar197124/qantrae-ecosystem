About QANTRAE Lite

QANTRAE Lite is a private, browser-based link analyzer built to help you inspect suspicious URLs before you open them. Analysis runs locally in your browser so you can review important warning signs without sending the link to a remote scanning service.

What it checks

  • Protocol safety and suspicious URL schemes
  • Domain structure, look-alikes, spoofing, and obfuscation
  • Encoded content and hidden redirect clues
  • Login, billing, verification, and scam language
  • Dangerous file extensions and double-extension tricks

What it does not do

  • It does not open the URL for you
  • It does not contact a remote server to perform the Lite scan
  • It does not guarantee that any link is completely safe
  • It does not store your scan history
  • It does not replace your judgment when something still feels suspicious
Privacy by design: QANTRAE Lite performs its link analysis locally in your browser session. The URL you paste into the Lite scanner is not sent elsewhere by this app for cloud analysis.

Privacy

QANTRAE Lite is designed around a simple principle: inspect the link without exposing the link. The Lite scanner performs its analysis locally in your browser instead of sending your pasted URL to a remote scanning service.

What stays private

  • No account is required to use the Lite scanner
  • Your pasted scan text is not uploaded by QANTRAE Lite for cloud analysis
  • No remote lookup is required for the core Lite scan
  • QANTRAE Lite does not build a scan-history profile from your checks
  • You remain in control of whether you open, copy, or ignore the link
Important: A privacy-first local scan can reveal suspicious patterns, but no scanner can guarantee that a link is safe. Treat the result as decision support, not permission to click blindly.

Transparency

QANTRAE Lite is built to show you why a link deserves attention instead of hiding the decision behind a mystery score. The scanner looks for visible URL patterns and explains the signals it finds in plain language.

What the scanner can show

  • Suspicious domain structure and look-alike naming
  • Encoded characters, punycode, shorteners, and direct IP addresses
  • Login, payment, verification, urgency, and phishing-style wording
  • Redirect clues and suspicious path or file patterns
  • Readable findings, risk indicators, and suggested next steps
Clear limits: QANTRAE Lite analyzes the information visible in the URL and its local rules. It does not claim certainty, and it does not hide that limitation from you. Stop. Think. Scan.
